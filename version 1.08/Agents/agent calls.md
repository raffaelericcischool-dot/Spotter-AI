**nutrition agent**:Use for meals, macros, hydration, fuelling and recovery nutrition, and packaged food or barcode questions.
**sleep agent**:Use for sleep hours, deep/REM, awakenings, HRV, resting heart rate and overnight recovery analysis against the athlete's baseline.
**technical agent**:Use for training load, technique, session structure, progression, overtraining and injury-risk signs, and gear or practice logistics.
**scheduling agent**:Use for planning training, rest days and appointments around the athlete's constraints, load and recovery.
**news agent**:Use for current news and updates about the athlete's sport, events and topics, with dated sources.
**ethical supervisor**:Use on every draft reply before it is sent, to approve or reject it for safety, sources, privacy and scope.
**mental agent**:Use for stress, motivation, confidence, performance pressure and burnout warning signs, including any crisis signal.
